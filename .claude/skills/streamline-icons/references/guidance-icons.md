# Guidance icon names

All 360 icons of Streamline's Guidance set as mirrored by Iconify (prefix `guidance`), grouped by Iconify's categories. Use them as `guidance:<name>`. Checked against `https://api.iconify.design/collection?prefix=guidance` and `@iconify-json/guidance` 1.2.2 on 25 September 2026. Streamline's own page lists 366 icons, so a few may exist only on streamlinehq.com.

Upstream quirks to know before you pick:

- **Left and right arrows are swapped.** `left-arrow` and `left-2-short-arrow` are drawn pointing right; `right-arrow` and `right-2-short-arrow` point left. `left-angle-arrow` turns up and to the right; `right-angle-arrow` turns down and to the left. Up, down and diagonal arrows match their names. Check every arrow visually.
- **Misspelt names are the real ids:** `accesible-restroom`, `elevator-accesible`, `changin-room-disabled`, `changin-room-family`, `changin-room-man`, `changin-room-woman`.
- **Filled exceptions:** every icon is a `currentColor` line drawing with an inherited 1 px stroke, except `no-drinking-water` (drawn as filled outlines) and `drinking-fountain` (mixes a filled shape with strokes). A `strokeWidth` override does not thicken the filled parts.
- `lockers` is drawn as a key, `time` as an hourglass, `24-hours` as a "24h" roundel, `customs` as a bag under a magnifier.
