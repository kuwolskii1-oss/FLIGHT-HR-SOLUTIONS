## 4. What the company actually does

### 4.1 Website positioning (consulting only)
The site presents a pure consultancy: "Your Partner for optimized engines management", built around **jet-engine shop visit management for airlines and leasing business**, with advisory, analysis and a **Military & Government Programs** line. The full service taxonomy is in `02-website-content-inventory.md`.

Capability list by engine family (Services page): CFM56-3 / -5 / -7, LEAP-1A / -1B / -1C; IAE V2500-A1 / -A5 / -D5; Pratt & Whitney PW4000-94 / -100 / -112; Honeywell APU 131-9A / -9B / -9D and turboprop TPE331 / T76 and TFE731.

### 4.2 Swiss Aerospace Cluster directory (verbatim core competences)
"Flight Hour Solution GmbH is a Switzerland-based aviation consulting company specializing in jet engine management and technical advisory services. With decades of combined experience, we support airlines, lessors, MROs and governmental operators worldwide. Our expertise covers shop visit management, contract negotiation, cost forecasting, documentation audits, and engine performance optimization, delivering transparency, reliability and measurable savings across every phase of engine operations."

Core competences listed: Full Engine Lifecycle Management · Shop Visit Management & Oversight · Engine Overhaul & Repair Contract Negotiation · Technical Documentation & Compliance Audits · Material Management & Scrap Rate Analysis · On-Wing Troubleshooting & Performance Advisory · Training & Knowledge Transfer · Predictive Maintenance & Data Analytics · Warranty & Invoice Review · Borescope & Test Cell Result Analysis · Insurance & Lease Contract Negotiation Support.

### 4.3 LinkedIn reality in 2026 (trading and sourcing)
Every company post since February 2026 is an **engine or aircraft transaction**: CFM56-3B2, CFM56-3C1, CFM56-7B26, Trent 772B and PT6A engines offered for sale; a completed Trent sale; a request for two 737-800s on dry lease. One post states the offer plainly: "we provide sourcing, technical advisory and end-to-end engine sales support"; another: "We continue to support companies worldwide with engine sourcing, technical advisory and shop visit management. If you are looking to buy or sell engines or aircraft, feel free to contact us." A dedicated sales mailbox exists (sales@flighthoursolution.com). ASA (Aviation Suppliers Association) membership and the hashtag #AircraftParts point to parts distribution as well.

### 4.4 Interpretation
Three revenue lines are visible: (1) engine-management consulting and technical advisory, (2) engine trading, remarketing and sourcing, (3) aircraft sourcing and lease brokerage, with parts supply emerging. **The website shows only line 1.** The new site must decide how to present lines 2 and 3 (a "Assets & Engines available" section, an "Engine sourcing" service page, or a separate trading brand).

## 7. Brand-name confusion risk
"Flight Hour Services" is a well-known Airbus service brand abbreviated **FHS**, the same abbreviation the new site would naturally use. A German flight-simulator company trades as FLIGHTHOUR, and the company itself alternates between "Solution" and "Solutions". The new site should (a) use one spelling everywhere, (b) avoid leaning on "FHS" as a standalone mark, and (c) win the brand query with strong Organization schema, a consistent NAP (name, address, phone) and a Google Business Profile.

## 8. Data-quality notes and discrepancies
- Three different phone numbers are in circulation; the hero number changed in April 2026 while the WhatsApp link still uses the old one.
- The privacy policy is a TermsFeed template that names **Poland** as the governing country and refers to user accounts and purchases the site does not have. Swiss law (revised FADP, in force since Sept 2023) and GDPR (EU clients) both apply; an Impressum with the legal entity, UID and address is expected on a Swiss commercial site and is missing.
- The Elementor form's consent checkbox says data is processed "by Flight Hour Solution", but the legal entity (GmbH) is not named anywhere on the site.
- Copyright line reads "Flight Hour Solution © 2026" with no legal form.
- LinkedIn says "Flight Hour Solutions is officially a member of the ASA" (plural); the register says singular.
