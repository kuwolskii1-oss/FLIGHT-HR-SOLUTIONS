About Flight Hour Solution
Your Partner for optimized engines management.
Our services keeps you flying!
Our Team with more than 40 years experience in engineering, operation, supply chain and contracting for civil and military aviation business offers you expert consulting on all aspects of engine management. 
We are your reliable and trustworthy partner and support you in making the operation of your engines most efficient and sustainable. Every organization is different, so we customize our services to align with your specific goals and challenges. 
Why choose us?
Reasons to cooperate with us
Our proven expertise in jet engine shop visit management ensures that your fleet remains fully operational and mission-capable. We are committed to providing proactive solutions that help you anticipate risks, optimize performance and meet the rigorous demands of military and governmental aviation. Partner with us to streamline your engine operations and ensure long-term operational success. 
Specialized Expertise
Our team consists of highly skilled engineers and consultants with deep knowledge of jet engines, from maintenance and repair to operational optimization.
Tailored Solutions
We customize our consulting services to meet the specific needs of your jet engine fleet, ensuring maximum efficiency and compliance.
Technology-Driven
We stay at the forefront of technological advancements, using tools like predictive maintenance and data analytics to minimize engine downtime and prevent costly repairs.
Our Story
Founded in 2024, Flight Hour Solution was established by a group of seasoned aviation professionals with decades of experience in the jet engine services sector. Having worked extensively with airlines, MROs and engine manufacturers, our founders recognized the need for a consulting firm that combined deep technical expertise with a client-focused approach. With a passion for innovation and excellence, our team set out to create a company that would help clients optimize jet engine performance, extend engine life and embrace new technologies.
Although Flight Hour Solution is new, our team’s long-standing experience in the industry allows us to offer proven, reliable solutions tailored to the specific needs of each client. Since our inception, we’ve been committed to pushing the boundaries of efficiency, safety and sustainability in jet engine operations, providing expert guidance for businesses navigating the complexities of modern aviation.
