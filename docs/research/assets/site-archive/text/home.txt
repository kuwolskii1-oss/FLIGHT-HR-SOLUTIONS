About Flight Hour Solution
Join Our Team
Our Team with more than 40 years experience in engineering, operation, supply chain and contracting for civil and military aviation business offers you expert consulting on all aspects of engine management.
Founded in 2024, Flight Hour Solution was established by a group of seasoned aviation professionals with decades of experience in the jet engine services sector. Having worked extensively with airlines, MROs, and engine manufacturers, our founders recognized the need for a consulting firm that combined deep technical expertise with a client-focused approach.

Specialized Expertise
Our team consists of highly skilled engineers and consultants with deep knowledge of jet engines, from maintenance and repair to operational optimization.
Tailored Solutions
We customize our consulting services to meet the specific needs of your jet engine fleet, ensuring maximum efficiency and compliance.
Technology-Driven
We stay at the forefront of technological advancements, using tools like predictive maintenance and data analytics to minimize engine downtime and prevent costly repairs.
