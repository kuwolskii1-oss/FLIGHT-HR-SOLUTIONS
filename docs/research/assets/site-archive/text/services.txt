Military & Government Programs
At Flight Hour Solution, we specialize in providing comprehensive jet engine shop visit management tailored to the unique needs of governmental and military aviation sectors. With over 40 years of expertise, we offer reliable and efficient solutions to ensure that critical aviation assets remain mission-ready and operationally effective.  
Our team understands the complexities of military and government aviation programs, delivering tailored support across the full lifecycle of engine operations. From detailed maintenance forecasting to contract negotiation and post-repair evaluations, we help you optimize engine performance while minimizing downtime.  
Our Services for Military & Government Aviation: 
 Full Shop Visit Management for Military-Grade Jet Engines 
 Customized Maintenance Reserve Calculations & Claim Analysis
 Engine Overhaul & Repair Contract Negotiation 
 Engine Purchase, Lease Contract and Insurance Claims Support 
 Borescope and Test Cell Analysis for Operational Readiness 
 Post-Shop Visit Documentation Audits and Performance Reviews
