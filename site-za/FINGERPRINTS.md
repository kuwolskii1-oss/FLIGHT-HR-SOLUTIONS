# Fingerprints

The scroll-craft registry for this studio's builds. A new build must differ from every row on at
least four of the six dimensions (grammar, nav treatment, hero device, act-sequence shape, close
pattern, signature move). Rows are append-only. The rules live in the skill's
`references/uniqueness.md`.

## The registry

| Build | Grammar | Nav treatment | Hero device | Act-sequence shape | Close pattern | Signature move | World | Port |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| fhs-ch (Swiss site, `site/app`) | Filmic scroll-scrub journey on the home page, document pages behind it | Four visible links, one filled action, full-screen menu on mobile | Scrubbed generated film with chaptered copy | scrub, flow, flow, flow, flow, flow | Statement headline with two actions | The engine film scrubbed by scroll through five chapters | Dark hangar, generated photoreal stills, paper document pages | 3000 |
| fhs-za (South African site, `site-za/app`) | Chaptered editorial: six chapters on the home page, one idea per screen; document-pace door pages | Five doors and About as visible links with dropdowns, urgent action always on, accordion panel on mobile | Pinned bespoke SVG ident driven by scroll | pin, flow, pin (cued steps), flow, flow, flow, flow | One question, one button, the urgent action as a link | Scrolling flies the plane in the logo along the swoosh until it lands on the logo | Paper and navy, brand photography to follow (generated placeholders) | 4600 |

The two rows differ on grammar, hero device, act shape, close pattern and signature move (five of
six); they share the brand world on purpose.

## What is taken

- Scrubbed generated film as a home hero (fhs-ch).
- A scroll-drawn logo lock-up as the hero and signature move (fhs-za).
- Pinned cued steps as the "how we work" device (fhs-za).
- The one-question close with a single button (fhs-za).
