# Voice, rules and facts for all Flight Hour Solution copy

Read first: docs/research/07-recommendations-and-brief.md (positioning, sitemap, page briefs),
docs/research/01-company-profile.md (legal facts, people, timeline, credentials),
docs/research/02-website-content-inventory.md (the existing copy, service taxonomy in section 4),
docs/research/06-market-context-and-seo.md (sourced market facts, personas, keywords, copy rules
in sections 5.2 and 5.3), docs/research/05-competitor-benchmark.md (vocabulary, proof patterns),
docs/research/08-locomotive-design-blueprint.md section 8 (page choreography and component list).

Voice
- Plain, numerate British English. Short sentences. Say what happens, what it costs, who does it.
- Entity name: "Flight Hour Solution" in prose, "Flight Hour Solution GmbH" in legal lines and
  the footer. Never "Solutions", never "FHS" on its own.
- Headlines 8 words or fewer. Sub-paragraphs 25 words or fewer. Body paragraphs 60 words or fewer.
- No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, colon, full stop or parentheses.
- Banned words: world-leading, one-stop, holistic, seamless, elevate, unleash, next-gen,
  revolutionise, innovative, cutting-edge, passionate, solutions (as a noun for services), synergy.
- No invented statistics, no invented clients, no testimonials, no invented case studies. Numbers
  come only from docs/research with a source, or from the company's own verified facts.
- One label per call-to-action intent site-wide: primary "Talk to us" (href /contact),
  secondary "Send us a workscope" (href /contact#workscope). Do not invent other contact labels.
- Never claim certifications or memberships as verified unless 01 marks them verified. ISO 9001
  and the Aviation Suppliers Association membership are "claimed by the company, certificate to be
  published"; the Swiss Aerospace Cluster membership is verified.
- Do not write "over 40 years of expertise" or any years-of-experience figure for the company
  (founded September 2024). If team experience is mentioned, say "the team's experience in
  engine shops and airline engineering" without a number, and list it under clientToConfirm.
- Independence: state plainly that the company is not owned by an MRO, lessor or OEM and works
  for the client; reconcile with the sourcing line by disclosing when it acts as a broker
  (07 section 2.3, option 1: two labelled lines, Advisory and Assets).
- Every service page uses the deliverable taxonomy from 02 section 4 (shop visit management,
  workscope compilation and review, table inspection, borescope analysis, LLP back-to-birth
  audit, maintenance reserve calculations and claim analysis, contract negotiation, invoice
  review, scrap review, warranty management, ECM/EHM monitoring, teardown valuation and so on).
- Engine families and models exactly as in 02 section 4: CFM56-3/-5/-7, LEAP-1A/-1B/-1C,
  V2500-A1/-A5/-D5, PW4000-94/-100/-112, Honeywell APU 131-9A/-9B/-9D, TPE331/T76, TFE731.
- Market facts: use only figures tagged [F] or [S] in 06 section 1, with the source named.
- Legal: Impressum with GmbH, UID CHE-196.248.398, address, managing director; privacy notice
  under the Swiss FADP and GDPR naming actual processors (the site is served by Cloudflare through
  the Higgsfield hosting platform; the contact form sends an email; no analytics, no marketing
  cookies); cookie notice stating that only technically necessary storage is used.
- Contact: one phone number, taken from 02/07 (the footer number); email address from 02;
  WhatsApp only if 02 documents it; do not invent a second office.
- Names: managing director Hubert Nowosad (sole owner and managing director since July 2025 per 01).
  Do not name former co-owners as current staff.
- Insights articles: build on 06 section 1 facts and 06 section 4.3 topics; cite sources in the
  article's sources array; write like a technical note, not a blog post.
